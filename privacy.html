Privacy Policy

Kazembe CodeWorks · nexttrain.co.za

1. What Information Do We Collect?

We respect your privacy. Next Train is designed to be used without creating an account.

Optional sign-in: If you create an account, Google or Facebook may act as an identity provider. We receive a name, email, and optional photo so we can keep your profile. We do not post on your behalf.

Location Data: If you allow location, Next Train may pick the nearest station on the live board and in Trip Planner From when those fields are still empty. Coordinates stay on your device. We never transmit, track, or store your physical location on our servers.

Service diagnostics: To keep the app fast and crash-free, we collect diagnostic data such as device type, operating system, selected region, crash logs, and a first-party device id.

When you use Trip Planner, we may store origin, destination, region, device id, and (if you are signed in) your account id, to improve routing reliability. This is not used to market to you.

Voluntary Information: If you use the In-App Feedback tool, you may optionally provide your email or WhatsApp number. This is used only to reply to your specific query and is never used for marketing.

2. Third-Party Tracking & Cookies

To understand how commuters use the app and to keep the servers running, we integrate with industry-standard third-party services:

Analytics: We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to understand usage patterns (for example which routes are popular) and fix UX issues. These services may set their own cookies.

Advertising: We use third-party ad networks to help cover server costs. These networks may use cookies to serve ads.

Infrastructure: Our schedules and live alerts are distributed via secure, globally recognized cloud infrastructure providers.

3. Accounts

If you sign in, we store your display name, email, and optional photo from Google or Facebook. We also store the last time the app was open, your selected region and route, and the app version, so we can keep your profile working. We do not store GPS on the account. We do not publish to your Facebook or Google feed.

4. Your POPIA Rights (South Africa)

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), you have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you have voluntarily provided to us (such as an account or a feedback email).

To delete a commuter account: open Next Train → Options → Account → Delete account. You can also follow the steps at nexttrain.co.za/account-delete.html or email admin@nexttrain.co.za from the address on the account.
