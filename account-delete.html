Delete Account

You can remove a Next Train account in the app, or by email. We do not need Facebook to finish the request.

In the app

  1. Open Next Train and tap Options (the menu).
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete account, then confirm.

You are signed out. We remove the account after we receive the request.

By email

Send a message from the email on the account to admin@nexttrain.co.za. Write Please delete my Next Train account and include the name or Facebook/Google address you used.
